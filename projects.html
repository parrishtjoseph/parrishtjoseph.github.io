Joseph Parrish · Shelén Hughes Camacho · Amir Farid

A Love Letter to the Americas

A recital exploring identity, migration, memory, and belonging through opera, art song, spirituals, folk traditions, and popular music from across the Americas.

The project places North and South American musical traditions in conversation, inviting audiences to hear familiar repertoire through a broader cultural lens.
